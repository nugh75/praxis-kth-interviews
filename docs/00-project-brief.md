# Project Brief

## Working title

PRAXIS KTH Interviews: GenAI in teaching, learning, doctoral work, and academic practice.

## Rationale

The original PRAXIS study investigated GenAI in educational contexts through online questionnaires and open responses. The KTH follow-up keeps the same conceptual framework but changes the empirical setting and the data collection format.

The change to face-to-face interviews is methodologically important: it allows follow-up questions, examples of real practices, clarification of institutional context, and richer accounts of uncertainty, trust, and skepticism.

## Participants

Three KTH participant groups:

- teachers/faculty involved in teaching or supervision;
- doctoral students;
- students.

Participant descriptions must be generalized in all public or committed material. Avoid combinations such as exact department, course, role, nationality, and narrow research field when they could identify a person.

## Core comparison with original PRAXIS

Keep comparable:

- the six PRAXIS dimensions;
- the codebook logic;
- the focus on actual practices, perceived competence, support, expectations, trust, and concerns.

Adapt:

- the interview format;
- the KTH institutional context;
- doctoral/research workflows;
- face-to-face prompts and probes;
- questions about local guidance, assessment, supervision, research integrity, privacy, and tool access.

## Immediate outputs

1. Interview protocol.
2. Consent and participant information drafts.
3. KTH-adapted codebook.
4. Data governance rules.
5. Analysis plan and traceability structure.
