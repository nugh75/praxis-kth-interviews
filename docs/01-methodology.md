# Methodology

## Design

Qualitative semi-structured interview study.

The study replicates the PRAXIS analytical method while replacing the online questionnaire with face-to-face interviews. The goal is not to reproduce numeric survey measures, but to preserve conceptual comparability through the same PRAXIS dimensions and a traceable codebook.

## Research questions

1. How do KTH teachers/faculty, doctoral students, and students use GenAI in educational and academic practice?
2. How do participants describe their practical and theoretical readiness to use GenAI critically?
3. What forms of institutional, peer, technical, and pedagogical support do participants perceive as present or missing?
4. What changes do participants expect in teaching, assessment, supervision, research, and learning?
5. How do participants describe trust in GenAI systems, institutions, teachers, supervisors, students, and peers?
6. What concerns, limits, and resistance patterns emerge in the KTH context?

## Sampling strategy

Use purposive sampling across the three target groups:

- teachers/faculty;
- doctoral students;
- students.

Within each group, seek variation in disciplinary area, level of teaching/learning responsibility, and experience with GenAI. Avoid collecting unnecessary identifying details.

Suggested initial target:

- 6 to 8 teachers/faculty;
- 6 to 8 doctoral students;
- 8 to 12 students.

This is an initial qualitative target, not a statistical sample. Stop or extend according to analytic saturation and practical feasibility.

## Data collection

Interviews should be face-to-face, semi-structured, and normally last 35 to 60 minutes.

Recommended workflow:

1. Provide participant information sheet.
2. Confirm consent before recording.
3. Assign a participant ID before the interview.
4. Record audio only if consent is explicit.
5. Take brief field notes after the interview.
6. Transcribe.
7. De-identify.
8. Convert to corpus record.
9. Code with PRAXIS-KTH codebook.

## Interview language

Default interview language: English.

Italian notes may be used internally by the researcher, but participant-facing material should be available in English. Swedish translations may be needed depending on recruitment and KTH requirements.

## Unit of analysis

The primary unit of analysis is a meaningful interview segment: a statement, example, narrative episode, or exchange that can be coded under one or more PRAXIS dimensions.

The participant is not the coding unit. A single interview can include multiple coded segments across dimensions.

## Comparability rule

A segment should first be tested against the existing PRAXIS codebook. Add a KTH-specific subcode only when:

- the segment is recurrent across interviews;
- the existing subcodes do not capture the relevant meaning;
- the new subcode improves analytic precision;
- the decision is recorded in `logs/codebook-decision-log.md`.

## Evidence rule

Every analytic claim must be traceable:

`interview_id -> transcript segment -> code -> analytic memo -> article/report section`

Use de-identified IDs only.
