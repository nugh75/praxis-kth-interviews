# Interview Protocol

## Purpose and source

This short interview guide adapts the PRAXIS survey questions from
`/home/nugh75/praxis-survey-live/backend/app/questions.py` into a qualitative,
semi-structured format for KTH interviews.

Target duration: maximum 20 minutes.

The interview includes 16 main questions: two short profile questions, two
questions for each PRAXIS dimension, and two closing questions.

PRAXIS dimensions:

- `P`: Practice patterns
- `R`: Readiness beliefs
- `A`: Adequacy of support
- `X`: eXpectations
- `I`: Interpersonal and Institutional trust
- `S`: Skepticisms

Default interview language: English. The interviewer may translate or clarify
orally when needed, but participant-facing written materials should remain
consistent with the approved study materials.

Use this as a flexible guide, not as a questionnaire to administer verbatim.
Ask the 16 main questions. Use probes only when a response is too brief or when
clarification is needed.

## Timing

- Setup and consent reminder: 2 minutes
- General profile: 2 minutes
- `P`, `R`, `A`: 3 minutes each
- `X`, `I`, `S`: 2 minutes each
- Closing: 1 minute

## Interview setup

Opening script:

> Thank you for taking part. I am studying how people at KTH use and make sense
> of generative AI in teaching, learning, research, and academic work. This is
> not a test of your knowledge. I am interested in concrete experiences,
> examples, doubts, expectations, and the institutional context around AI use.
> You can skip any question and stop the interview at any time.

Before starting:

- confirm informed consent;
- confirm whether audio recording is allowed;
- assign the participant ID;
- remind the participant not to name colleagues, students, supervisors, courses,
  departments, labs, or projects where this is not necessary;
- do not collect unnecessary personal identifiers.

## Short Profile

Purpose: describe the participant only at a generalized level. Do not ask for
the original survey's anonymous code based on a family surname and birth day.

Source survey areas: profile questions from both `studenti` and `insegnanti`.

1. In general terms, what is your current role at KTH?
2. What kinds of teaching, learning, research, supervision, or study activities
   are part of your current work?

Optional probes:

- faculty, doctoral student, student;
- broad disciplinary area only;
- coursework, thesis work, supervision, research, teaching, administration.

## P. Practice Patterns

Source survey items: `Uso_Quotidiano_Check`, `Uso_Studio_Check`,
`Uso_Didattica_Check`, `Strumenti_Usi`, `Strumenti_Regolari`, `Scopi_Uso`,
`Attivita_Uso`, `P_Cercare`, `P_Scrivere`, `P_Compiti`, `P_Sintesi`,
`P_Idee`.

3. Do you currently use generative AI tools in your academic or work life?
4. Can you describe one recent concrete example of how you used, or deliberately
   avoided using, an AI tool?

Optional probes:

- Which tool did you use?
- What did you ask it to do?
- What did you do with the output?
- Was this for studying, teaching, research, supervision, writing, coding,
  feedback, assessment, summarizing, or brainstorming?

## R. Readiness Beliefs

Source survey items: `Comp_Pratica`, `Comp_Teorica`, `R_Troubleshoot`,
`R_Autoappr`, `R_ValutaOutput`.

5. How confident do you feel in the practical use of AI tools?
6. How do you decide whether an AI-generated answer is correct, reliable, or
   useful?

Optional probes:

- practical tool use;
- theoretical understanding of AI;
- learning by trial and error;
- checking accuracy, bias, incompleteness, or usefulness.

## A. Adequacy of Support

Source survey items: `Formazione_Adeguata`, `A_Risorse`, `A_Supporto`,
`A_LineeGuida`, `A_Pratica`.

7. What guidance, training, or resources about AI use are available to you at
   KTH?
8. Are the rules and support around AI use clear and adequate for your needs?

Optional probes:

- KTH-level, course-level, programme-level, or supervisor guidance;
- peer support;
- approved tools or licences;
- privacy, student data, confidential material, unpublished research;
- what support is missing.

## X. eXpectations

Source survey items: `Impatto_Studio`, `Impatto_Didattica_Gen`,
`Impatto_Mia_Didattica`, `X_Settore`, `X_Disponibilita`, `X_Valutazione`,
`X_Preparazione`.

9. How do you expect AI to change studying, teaching, supervision, or research
   at KTH?
10. Do you expect assessment, exams, assignments, or thesis work to change
    because of AI?

Optional probes:

- changes in your own practice;
- doctoral education;
- short-term and long-term changes;
- positive, negative, and ambiguous expectations.

## I. Interpersonal and Institutional Trust

Source survey items: `Fiducia_Integrazione`,
`Fiducia_Insegnanti_Competenza`, `Fiducia_Studenti_Responsabile`,
`I_Utilita`, `I_Affidabilita`, `I_Sviluppatori`, `I_Istituzione`.

11. When do you trust, or not trust, AI-generated output?
12. How much do you trust students, teachers, supervisors, peers, KTH, or tool
    providers to use or manage AI responsibly?

Optional probes:

- reliability of AI-generated answers;
- responsible use by students or peers;
- preparedness of teachers or supervisors;
- institutional governance;
- tool providers and developers.

## S. Skepticisms

Source survey items: `Preocc_Integrazione`, `Preocc_Educazione`,
`Preocc_Compagni`, `Preocc_Studenti_Uso`, `Non_Uso_Scopi`,
`Non_Uso_Attivita`, `S_Integrita`, `S_Dipendenza`, `S_Privacy`, `S_Bias`.

13. What concerns you most about AI in education, research, or academic life?
14. Are there activities where AI should not be used, or should remain
    human-led?

Optional probes:

- academic integrity, authorship, plagiarism, hidden AI use;
- overdependence or loss of critical thinking;
- privacy, personal data, confidential information, unpublished research;
- bias, discrimination, unfairness.

## Closing

15. What would a good institutional response from KTH look like?
16. Is there anything important about AI at KTH that I did not ask?

Close by reminding the participant how the data will be handled, how withdrawal
works, and whom to contact for questions.

Do not treat the interview answers as survey scores. Use them only as
qualitative material and code them against the PRAXIS dimensions when supported
by the transcript.
