# Codebook Adaptation

## Principle

The KTH study starts from the PRAXIS codebook used in the original project. The six main dimensions remain stable:

- `P`: Practice patterns
- `R`: Readiness beliefs
- `A`: Adequacy of support
- `X`: eXpectations
- `I`: Interpersonal & Institutional trust
- `S`: Skepticisms

The interview format makes it possible to capture richer examples than the online questionnaire. This does not automatically justify new codes. New KTH-specific subcodes should be introduced only when they capture a recurrent pattern not already covered by the original codebook.

## KTH adaptation candidates

These are candidate extensions to test during coding, not final categories.

### P-KTH1. Research workflow integration

Use of GenAI in research workflows: literature scanning, coding, data analysis support, paper drafting, peer review preparation, grant writing, lab documentation, or experiment planning.

Keep separate from generic writing support when the segment specifically concerns research practice.

### P-KTH2. Supervision and doctoral work

Use of GenAI in doctoral supervision, thesis planning, supervisor feedback, doctoral course work, or independent research training.

### A-KTH1. Institutional rule clarity

Perceived clarity or ambiguity of KTH-level, school-level, department-level, or course-level rules about GenAI.

### A-KTH2. Approved infrastructure and tool access

Availability, absence, or uneven access to sanctioned tools, secure platforms, licenses, or institutional technical infrastructure.

### X-KTH1. Assessment redesign

Expectation that exams, assignments, oral assessment, project work, or thesis evaluation must change because of GenAI.

### I-KTH1. Trust in institutional governance

Trust or distrust in KTH, departments, programs, or course teams to manage GenAI responsibly.

### S-KTH1. Research integrity and authorship

Concerns about authorship, plagiarism, hidden AI use, reproducibility, responsibility for claims, or integrity in research outputs.

### S-KTH2. Confidentiality and data protection

Concerns about entering unpublished research, student data, interview data, personal information, or confidential material into AI tools.

## Decision rule

For each candidate subcode:

1. Collect at least three clear examples across at least two participants, unless the pattern is rare but analytically critical.
2. Check whether an existing PRAXIS subcode is sufficient.
3. Record the decision in `logs/codebook-decision-log.md`.
4. Update `codebook/praxis-kth-codebook.yaml`.
