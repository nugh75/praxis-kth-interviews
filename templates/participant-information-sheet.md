# Participant Information Sheet - Draft

## Study

PRAXIS KTH Interviews: Generative AI in teaching, learning, doctoral work, and academic practice.

## Purpose

The study explores how people at KTH use and understand generative AI in academic contexts. The interview will ask about concrete uses, perceived competence, institutional support, expectations, trust, and concerns.

## What participation involves

You will take part in one face-to-face interview lasting approximately 35 to 60 minutes.

With your permission, the interview may be audio-recorded for transcription. You may decline recording and still take part if the study procedure allows note-based participation.

## Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may skip any question or stop the interview at any time.

## Data handled

The study may collect:

- interview audio, if you consent;
- transcript text;
- field notes;
- generalized role information;
- de-identified excerpts for analysis.

Identifiable data will not be included in public outputs.

## Confidentiality

The research team will remove or generalize names, exact roles, course identifiers, departments, research projects, and other details that could identify you or others.

## AI tools

Identifiable interview data will not be uploaded to third-party AI tools unless this has been explicitly approved by the project governance route and explained to participants.
